# Strange Invention — Concept Brief
**Brand:** strangeinvention.com  
**Updated:** 2026-09-24 (Ooger/Doc dig)  
**Status:** Waitlist live elsewhere; this file is canon for characters + loop

## One-liner
A NeverEnding Experimental Warehouse: interactive science museum + discovery shop + Rube Goldberg art + patent archive + cartoon universe. Visitors play and learn; selected artifacts are purchasable. Commerce = “solidifying context.”

## Characters

### Doc
Primary host/inventor. Aloof, confident, theatrical mad-scientist/curator. Oblivious to Ooger’s suffering; dangerous experiments are routine. Issues commands. Does not usually call visitors “hoomans.”

### Ooger
Sarcastic sidekick. Calls people “hoomans.” Background chaos: repairs, disasters, comic injury. Translates Doc’s science into plain language. Checkout micro-fee can be “Ooger’s Wage” / “Ooger’s Snack Fund.”

### Adjacent duo (same function)
**Professor** (enthusiastic scientist + warehouse news desk) + **Igor** (chaotic dimensional fetch assistant). Not confirmed renames of Doc/Ooger.

### Infinite Guide (optional layer)
Bureaucratic concierge; deadpan meta-mockery; voice/search/checkout.

## Loop
Lobby choice (explore / shop / daily drop) → endless warehouse exploration → investigate (patent/lore/real vs fictional vs buyable) → entertainment (ads, Rube Goldberg, voice, shake-to-breach) → commerce reveal (“Inquire About Stability” → “Solidify Context”) → in-world checkout overlay → retention (Entropy, daily drops).

## Inventory principle
Mostly free edu-tainment; buyables are souvenirs (~80/20 or 70% patents / 10% fiction / 10% shop).

## MVP heroes
Saluting Device, Plow Gun, Face Mask (+ Quantum Lighter, Unstable Crystal).
