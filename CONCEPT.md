# Strange Invention — concept

**Brand:** Strange Invention / [strangeinvention.com](https://strangeinvention.com)  
**Also written:** Strangeinvention

## One-liner

A **NeverEnding Warehouse** that is also a mad-scientist lab: shopping = gamified exploration; inventions as experiments; **edu-tainment carries commerce**.

## Unified idea

1. **NeverEnding Warehouse** — Lab floor + aisles forever. Discovery hotspots, forbidden curiosity, inventions as experiments you explore — not a sterile catalog. Full 3D build is future; landing only hints at the UX.
2. **Edu-tainment** — Learn the weird science as you browse. Entertainment frame is OK; health claims and fake commerce are not.
3. **E-commerce (gated)** — Real drops when ready. **Portal Keys** / limited releases later when demand shows. Waitlist / early access first. No fake inventory, SKUs, or buy-now for things that don’t ship yet.
4. **Hosts / duo**
   - **Doc** — Chaotic Professor / Mad Scientist host; reverse-psychology energy (“don’t touch that”).
   - **Ooger** — Companion / sidekick. Keep light on the landing; exact Ooger bio TBD (names have evolved).

## What this repo is

Waitlist landing only. Not the full platform. Tone: humanized, inventive, playful-but-clear (Bopen/Nifty-ish). Not AeraSell. Not Albany AI Guy.

## Legal / honesty rails

- Waitlist / early access only
- No fake live shop, fake patent counts, fake SKUs, or sales metrics
- Fiction/entertainment vibe OK; real commerce stays behind the waitlist
