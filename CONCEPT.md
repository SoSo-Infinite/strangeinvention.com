# Strange Invention — concept

**Brand:** Strange Invention / [strangeinvention.com](https://strangeinvention.com)  
**Also written:** Strangeinvention

## One-liner

A **NeverEnding Warehouse** that is also a mad-scientist lab: shopping = gamified exploration; inventions as experiments; **edu-tainment carries commerce**. Visitors aren’t just buying — they’re **solidifying context**.

## Unified idea

1. **NeverEnding Warehouse** — Lab floor + aisles forever. Discovery hotspots, forbidden curiosity, inventions as experiments you explore — not a sterile catalog. Full 3D build is future; landing only hints at the UX.
2. **Edu-tainment** — Learn the weird science as you browse. Entertainment frame is OK; health claims and fake commerce are not.
3. **E-commerce (gated)** — Real drops when ready. **Portal Keys** / limited releases later when demand shows. Waitlist / early access first. No fake inventory, SKUs, or buy-now for things that don’t ship yet.
4. **Hosts / duo**
   - **Doc** — Aloof, confident, theatrical mad-scientist/curator. Oblivious to Ooger’s suffering; treats dangerous experiments as routine. Issues commands. Does **not** usually call visitors “hoomans.”
   - **Ooger** — Sarcastic sidekick. Calls people **“hoomans.”** Background chaos (repairs, disasters, comic injury). Plain-language translator of Doc’s grandiose science. Checkout micro-fee can be framed as **Ooger’s Wage** / **Ooger’s Snack Fund**.
   - **Adjacent (same structure):** Professor + Igor — enthusiastic news-desk scientist + chaotic dimensional fetch assistant. Same duo function; not confirmed renames.
   - **Optional layer:** Infinite Guide — bureaucratic concierge; deadpan meta-mockery; voice/search/checkout.

## Loop (full warehouse, later)

Lobby choice → explore hotspots → investigate (patent / lore / real vs fictional vs buyable) → ads & Rube Goldberg spectacle → “Inquire About Stability” → “Solidify Context” checkout overlay → Entropy / daily drops for retention.

## Inventory principle (later)

Mostly free discovery/edu-tainment; buyables are souvenirs (~80/20). Real vs fictional vs purchasable must look different. Third-party brands: authentic packaging only; reseller disclaimer.

## MVP hero seeds (later)

Saluting Device, Plow Gun, Face Mask (+ Quantum Lighter, Unstable Crystal).

## What this repo is

Waitlist landing only. Not the full platform. Tone: humanized, inventive, playful-but-clear (Bopen/Nifty-ish). Not AeraSell. Not Albany AI Guy.

## Legal / honesty rails

- Waitlist / early access only
- No fake live shop, fake patent counts, fake SKUs, or sales metrics
- Fiction/entertainment vibe OK; real commerce stays behind the waitlist
