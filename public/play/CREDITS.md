# Warehouse preview credits

This walk is a private preview. The people and gadgets in it are placeholders.

## Characters

Doc and Ooger are original placeholder kitbashes made from simple shapes in this project. They are not final art.

Doc, when the real art lands, is tall and thin, with wild white hair, a clean-shaven face, a long teal and brown coat with glowing cyan trim, and a gadget belt. He does not carry a staff. He is not a doctor.

Ooger, when the real art lands, is short, hairless, pale grey-green, and pot-bellied, with huge droopy eyes, one snaggle tooth, bandages, a tool belt, and a small glow of green slime that stays kid-safe.

No Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Haven, or ambientCG files are shipped in this preview. Those libraries are CC0 when a later pass needs them. This build uses code-built boxes, not downloaded meshes.

## Pictures and plaques

Shelf cards are type, not scanned drawings. U.S. patent texts named on the plaques are old and expired. Their drawings were not copied into the repo, because a picture is only added when its source is obvious and public domain.

## Shop links

Some shelf cards link out to eBay search pages. Those pages are starter picks for the owner to replace with real listings. The preview does not host eBay pictures. Strange Invention earns a commission if a visitor buys through a tracked link, and the card says so.
